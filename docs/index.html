Using the dashboard

Clickable boxes: “Opportunities with tagged PSE” shows Partner Opportunities with a populated PSE that is missing from the Team, Splits, or both. “Partner but no tagged PSE” shows a blank PSE whose proposed Partner Owner is missing from the Team, Splits, or both. “All Opportunities to update” also includes a blank PSE field when the Owner is already on both the Team and Splits. Counts follow your search and Fiscal Quarter–Year, Type, Segment, Region, and PSE Status filters. Update all and Export CSV use the selected box.

What appears: Closed Opportunities (Stage 08 - Closed) in scope with a Partner and at least one missing step. Fully configured Opportunities count in the totals and leave the update list.

Refresh data: Reads the latest Salesforce records and rebuilds the dashboard. It does not populate fields or add Team members or Splits.

Update in Salesforce: Applies this process to that Opportunity after confirmation.

Update all N in Salesforce: Applies this process to all matching Opportunities across all pages, using your current search and Fiscal Quarter–Year, Type, Segment, Region, and PSE Status selections. Each Opportunity uses its own existing PSE or its own Partner’s Owner.

Existing records: The dashboard rechecks Salesforce before each stage and adds missing entries. It preserves populated PSE values and existing Team and Split entries.

Fiscal Quarter–Year filter: Uses Opportunity Close Date and Zendesk’s February–January fiscal calendar: Q1 Feb–Apr, Q2 May–Jul, Q3 Aug–Oct, Q4 Nov–Jan. For example, November 2026 through January 2027 is FY2027 Q4. Select one or multiple quarters; clearing the selection shows all quarters.

PSE Status filter: Select Active, Inactive, or both. For a blank PSE field, status refers to the Partner Owner proposed as PSE. Card counts, list results, Update all, and Export CSV follow this filter.

Inactive or unavailable users: User status is displayed; the new flow does not exclude inactive users. An unresolved Owner or a Salesforce validation error is reported and the Opportunity stays incomplete. A failed step stops later additions for that Opportunity. Retry continues from the remaining missing steps.

Return to your work: Click Close or press Escape. Your search, filters, and dashboard position are preserved.
